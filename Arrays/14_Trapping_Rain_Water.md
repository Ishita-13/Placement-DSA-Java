# Trapping Rain Water

## Difficulty
Hard

## Pattern
Two Pointers

## Key Idea
Water at an index depends on the smaller of the left-max and right-max.

## Approach
Use `left`, `right`, `leftMax`, and `rightMax`.

## Time Complexity
O(n)

## Space Complexity
O(1)

## Key Trick
Smaller boundary decides the trapped water.
